# Onboarding a New Employee

## Before Day One
- Send offer letter
- Order laptop and equipment
- Create accounts
  - Email
  - Slack
  - GitHub

## Day One
- First, welcome and office tour
- Then, IT setup and account verification
- Finally, meet the team

## First Month
- Assign a mentor
- Complete training modules
  - Security training
  - Product training
- 30-day check-in with manager
